# Trade atlas: every playbook setup by instrument, timeframe, hour and bias (history)

**1,068,614 trades**, 2021-01-03 → 2026-09-30, OANDA bid/ask with the real spread, replayed by the playbook's own engine. 29 setups (default parameters) × 3 instruments × 5 timeframes (15 of 15 jobs finished). Standard exit (partial at +1R, runner trailed), one trade at a time per setup, entries at all hours 20:00 → 15:50 NY, news blackouts on.

**The bias rule is OFF here.** Each trade's bias at entry is classed exactly as the live rule would: aligned (side × score ≥ 0.25), neutral (|score| below it) or counter. The live playbook trades only the aligned ones.

**How to read.** avg R = average result per trade in units of its risk, after the spread. 95% range = where the true average probably lies (5-day blocks of trading days). Pips = R × the stop distance (gold 1 pip = $0.10; NDQ/US30 1 pip = 1 point), per initial unit. Halves split at 2023-11-17. Brackets = under 30 trades, don't trust.

✅ **stands out**: ≥ 100 trades, 95% range above 0, both halves positive, positive at 2× costs. ⛔ **clearly bad**: ≥ 100 trades and the whole 95% range below 0.

**This is a map, not a test.** 5,172 slices were screened (4,688 with ≥ 100 trades); about 59 could stand out by luck alone. **17 stand out, 2252 are clearly bad.** Anything here earns a registered test and the untouched 2016–2020 data before it trades. Every slice is in `cells.csv` (filter it yourself).
