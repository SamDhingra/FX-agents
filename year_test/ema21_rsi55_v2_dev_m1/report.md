# Rule test: ema21_rsi55_v2 on dev_m1

Base rule (frozen before testing): candle closes above EMA21 (previous close at/below it) with RSI(14) > 55 → buy at the ask on the next minute; exit at the bid on the next minute after the first close below EMA21. Long only, no stop. R = ATR(14) of that timeframe at entry. Real OANDA bid/ask (one spread per round trip, already in every result); 2× costs pays that spread once more. Versions: 24h = as written (forced out before closures > 60 min); day = entries 02:00–15:30 NY, flat 15:50 NY. Volume rvol≥1 = the signal candle's tick volume at least the median of the same time-of-day candle over the previous 20 days. Add = one extra equal unit, once, on a later close above EMA21 with 58 < RSI ≤ 70 while above the entry price; R is per initial unit, so an added trade's R counts both units.

Trades span 2024-10-01 → 2026-09-30. Pass = ≥100 trades, avg R > 0, 95% day-block range above 0, avg R > 0 at 2× costs. **0 of 48 variants pass.** With 48 variants, about 1 could pass by luck alone, so anything that passes here goes to the holdout once, unchanged, before it counts.
