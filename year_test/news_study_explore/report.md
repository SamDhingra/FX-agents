Descriptive. What gold, NDQ and US30 did around the scheduled USD releases the bot currently blacks out (no entries 30 min before and after): FOMC 14:00, CPI / NFP / PPI 08:30 NY. **The data after 2023-11-17 and all of 2016–2020 were not read**, so they are still clean for the registered test (step 2) and its confirmation (step 3).

Units: moves in **ATR15** (the average 15-minute candle range just before the window), so instruments and years compare. Ranges are a bootstrap over release days (instruments on the same day count together). **\*** marks a 95% range that excludes 0 — with this many cells, about 1 in 20 does that by luck alone; the count is at the end.

Not available: the released numbers and forecasts (the surprise). The historical calendar has dates and times only, and the live feed has no real-time actual at release, so a rule here can only use prices.

## How many cells look significant, vs luck

34 of 294 directional cells have a 95% range that excludes 0; about 15 would by luck alone (they are also correlated with each other). A cell is a lead for step 2 only if it is large enough to pay the spread, consistent across instruments or types, and makes sense — not because it has a star.
